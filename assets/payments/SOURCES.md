# Payment marks

Local SVG marks for Mada, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, and stc pay are bundled with the theme so the footer does not depend on external image servers. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners.

- Mada: official brand artwork as distributed in the Mada branding guidelines; vector mirrored at Wikimedia Commons (`File:Mada Logo.svg`).
- stc pay: original stc pay vector artwork; vector mirrored at Wikimedia Commons (`File:Stc pay.svg`).
